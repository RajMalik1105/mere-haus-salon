Meet The Founders

The Story of
Mére Haus.

Mére Haus, meaning "Mother's House", is a mother and daughter salon created by Amanda and Justine. Built around our shared love of beautiful hair, Mére Haus combines Amanda's years of experience and signature techniques with Justine's passion for colour, extensions and relaxation.

Together, we've created a space that feels personal, welcoming and relaxed, while delivering highly skilled, modern hair. Mére Haus is our home, our craft and our way of creating a salon experience that feels a little different from the rest.

Amanda

CO-FOUNDER & MASTER STYLIST

GET TO KNOW HER ↓

With years of hands-on experience and a passion for bespoke color work, Amanda brings an unparalleled level of artistry to every transformation. She works directly with factory sources to ensure the highest quality sourcing, cuticle integrity, and custom color matching for the Mére Haus collection.

Justine

CO-FOUNDER & EXTENSION SPECIALIST

GET TO KNOW HER ↓

Combining technical precision with deep industry knowledge, Justine has been instrumental in developing Mére Haus's signature installation techniques. Her expertise ensures that every custom order is flawlessly executed to perfectly match the client's natural texture, density, and lifestyle.

THE MÉRE HAUS EXPERIENCE

A Moment to Switch Off.

At Mére Haus, your appointment is about more than just beautiful hair. It is a space designed for you to truly switch off and be looked after.

Led by Justine's signature calming touch, our appointments incorporate incredible massages, soothing scalp rituals, and dedicated head spa treatments. Every detail is thoughtfully curated to bring a sense of deep relaxation to your transformation.

RESERVE YOUR EXPERIENCE

Custom-Tailored For Your Hair

At Mére Haus, we source and match extensions directly to your natural color and texture, eliminating the need for post-installation coloring.

By working directly with our factory, we provide bespoke custom orders and rigorously test our hair for real-world humidity performance.

The result is a flawlessly personalized extension transformation designed specifically for you and the way you actually live.

Mére Haus Extensions

Created from experience, backed by knowledge.

Mére Haus Extensions are born from years of hands-on expertise and a commitment to uncompromising quality. We travel directly to our factory in China to oversee every step of the process—from ethical sourcing to custom color development.

Crafted from 100% virgin human hair with intact cuticles, our collection is designed for flawless, individualized transformations. We don't just install extensions; we understand the hair itself.

Mére Haus Extensions is backed by a 12-month guarantee.
